Sources & coverage

Map data: OpenFreeMap, OpenMapTiles and OpenStreetMap. Offline outline: Natural Earth. Style adapted from OpenFreeMap Positron / OpenMapTiles, derived from CartoDB basemaps by Stamen and Paul Norman; Nori modifies colors, labels and overlays. Style licenses.

Place/address search: Photon. Searches are submitted only when you press Search, with caching and a cooldown. Avoid submitting confidential addresses. Weather requests send selected coordinates to Open-Meteo.

Open-Meteo: current model weather, non-commercial free-service terms. USGS: M2.5+ earthquakes, past day. NASA EONET: selected natural-event observations, not official warnings. NWS: US watches, warnings and advisories; products without mapped geometry remain unmapped.

FAA layers cover the United States only. Facility grids do not authorize flight. Always check official FAA sources and current restrictions. No flight clearance or worldwide legal coverage is implied. Federal ground restrictions use USGS PAD-US 4.1 land data with NPS, USFWS and wilderness policies. Source dates are shown in each feature; land datasets are not live closures or cadastral surveys.

China weather: CMA reports via WMO, recent reports and updates including cancellations, not a complete active-warning inventory. Regional reference markers do not identify hazard locations or affected boundaries. The feed is capped; cached snapshots show their age and stale status. Hong Kong Observatory warning signals cover Hong Kong only and keep their original signal meanings.

Unavailable layers are labeled, not replaced by simulated conditions. This map is not an emergency notification service or an official flight briefing.
